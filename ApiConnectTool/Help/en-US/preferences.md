# Preferences

1. Choose **Edit > Preferences...**.
2. Choose the production send warning policy: **Always warn**, **Warn once per session** or **Never warn**.
3. Set whether **Save Request As** and duplicate operations prompt for a name.
4. Close the window. Preferences are saved in your workspace.

Mark individual collection or global environments as production in **Edit > Environments...** or **Edit > Global Environments...**; with **Warn once per session**, each production environment is confirmed separately. Request-specific timeout, proxy and TLS settings are in the request's **Settings** tab. See [Environments and Variables](environments), [Requests](requests) and [Data and secrets](data-and-secrets).
